1. Average Price
Definition: Mean property price across all listings.

Formula: SUM(Price) / COUNT(Properties)

Purpose: Tracks overall market pricing trends.

Related Measure: Average Price

2. Price per Sqft
Definition: Normalized property price based on square footage.

Formula: SUM(Price) / SUM(Sqft)

Purpose: Allows comparison of properties of different sizes.

Related Measure: Price per Sqft

3. Growth %
Definition: Percentage change in property prices over time.

Formula: (Current Period Price – Previous Period Price) / Previous Period Price * 100

Purpose: Measures market momentum and volatility.

Related Measure: Growth %

4. Market Share by Property Type
Definition: Proportion of each property type in total listings.

Formula: COUNT(PropertyID by Type) / COUNT(All PropertyID)

Purpose: Identifies dominant property categories in the market.

Related Measure: Market Share

5. Net Revenue
Definition: Revenue generated after deducting costs.

Formula: SUM(Revenue) – SUM(Cost)

Purpose: Evaluates profitability of property transactions.

Related Measure: Net Revenue

6. Occupancy Rate (Optional)
Definition: Percentage of properties currently rented or sold.

Formula: Occupied Properties / Total Properties * 100

Purpose: Indicates demand and utilization efficiency.

Related Measure: Occupancy Rate
